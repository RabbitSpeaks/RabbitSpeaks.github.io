Dunderball Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14th, 2026

This policy explains what information the Dunderball mobile game ("the game") handles, and why. The game is made by Rabbit Speaks ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at rabbitxspeaks@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email, or any account. There's no sign-in.
  • Your high score and settings stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect information from your device to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads use your data, and you can change your choice at any time.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your high score, your settings (such as volume, haptics and graphics options) and your chosen style and background. This information is stored only on your device. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete your high score in Settings, and uninstalling the game deletes all of it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC:

  • A banner-style ad on the Game Over screen.
  • An optional rewarded video ad. You choose to watch it to continue a game, and the game always asks you first.

To serve, measure and improve ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped);
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the ads SDK.

Google may use this information to show you personalized ads, where you have allowed it, or non-personalized ads otherwise. Google handles this information under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses advertising data

Your choices

  • Consent: if you're in a region whose laws require consent for ad personalization, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form when it starts. Where your state's privacy law gives you the right to opt out, such as in some US states, the same form lets you do so.
  • Changing your choice: where these rules apply, a Privacy options button appears in the game's Settings menu. It reopens the consent form at any time.
  • Your advertising ID: on Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (usually Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you never have to watch one to keep playing a new game.

Game engine diagnostics (Unity)

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may collect limited technical diagnostics from the game, such as crash reports, performance data, device model and operating system version, to help keep the engine and the game stable. This data isn't used for advertising. See Unity's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the game, contact us and we'll help address it.

Data sharing and selling

We don't sell your information, and we don't receive any information that identifies you. The information described above is collected and processed by Google, for ads, and by Unity, for diagnostics, under their own policies.

Data retention and deletion

Information saved on your device stays there until you reset it in the game or uninstall the game. For information collected by Google or Unity, see their policies linked above for how long it's kept and how to request deletion. You can also contact us, and we'll help where we can.

Security

The game doesn't send your scores or settings anywhere. Information collected by Google's and Unity's SDKs is sent to them over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's store listing.
